The Origin Story

FROM BENIN CITY
TO THE WORLD.

Divine Joshua Ikubor was born May 1, 2000, in Benin City, Edo State — a city of ancient Benin Kingdom legacy that now pulses with one of Africa's most electrifying voices. Raised in a Christian household, Rema found his calling in church music before graduating to viral Instagram freestyles that shook the Nigerian music industry to its foundation.

In 2018, a freestyle posted to D'Prince's track "Gucci Gang" caught the attention of music executive D'Prince, leading to a deal with Jonzing World, a subsidiary of legendary producer Don Jazzy's Mavin Records. One year later, "Dumebi" erupted globally — earning a spot on Barack Obama's 2019 summer playlist and cementing Rema as Afrobeats' brightest young star.

His 2022 album Rave & Roses introduced the world to his signature Afrorave sound — a fearless collision of Afrobeats, trap, house, and dancehall. Its breakout single "Calm Down," remixed with Selena Gomez, peaked at #3 on the Billboard Hot 100 and reigned on the Billboard Afrobeats chart for a record 58 weeks. The 2024 follow-up, HEIS (Greek for "number one"), cemented his standing with a Grammy nomination — the first of many to come.
