The Origin Story

FROM BENIN
TO THE WORLD.

Divine Joshua Ikubor was born May 1, 2000, in Benin City, Edo State — a city pulsing with ancient Benin Kingdom legacy. Raised in a Christian household, Rema found his calling in church music before viral Instagram freestyles shook Nigeria's music industry to its core.

In 2018, a freestyle caught the ear of D'Prince, leading to a deal with Jonzing World, a subsidiary of Don Jazzy's legendary Mavin Records. "Dumebi" erupted globally in 2019 — landing on Barack Obama's summer playlist and announcing Rema as Afrobeats' brightest young star.

His 2022 album Rave & Roses introduced the world to Afrorave — a fearless collision of Afrobeats, trap, house, and dancehall. "Calm Down" ft. Selena Gomez peaked at #3 on the Billboard Hot 100. His 2024 follow-up HEIS (Greek for "number one") earned a Grammy nomination and a Coachella debut.
