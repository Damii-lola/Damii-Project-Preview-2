The Origin Story

FROM BENIN CITY
TO THE WORLD.

Divine Joshua Ikubor, born May 1, 2000, in Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria — the ancient seat of the Benin Kingdom, and now the birthplace of one of Africa's greatest musical exports. Raised in a devout Christian household, Rema first found his voice in church before viral Instagram freestyles set the entire internet on fire.

In 2019, a freestyle posted over D'Prince's "Gucci Gang" went viral, landing him a deal with Jonzing World — Don Jazzy's Mavin Records subsidiary. His debut EP's "Iron Man" appeared on Barack Obama's 2019 summer playlist, and "Dumebi" spread globally, announcing the world's introduction to Afrorave.

His 2022 debut album Rave & Roses redefined global Afrobeats. "Calm Down," remixed with Selena Gomez, hit #3 on the Billboard Hot 100, spent 58 weeks atop the Afrobeats chart, and crossed 1 billion YouTube views — the biggest Afrobeats song of all time. His 2024 album HEIS (Greek for "number one") earned his first Grammy nomination. The Rave Lorde is just getting started.
